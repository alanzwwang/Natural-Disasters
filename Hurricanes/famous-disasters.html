🌀 Famous Hurricanes

These are some of the most famous — and most powerful — hurricanes in history. Each one taught people something new about how to stay safe!

🇺🇸 The Great Galveston Hurricane, USA (1900)

The deadliest natural disaster in US history! In September 1900, a Category 4 hurricane slammed into Galveston, Texas, pushing a wall of water 4.5 metres high over the island city. Afterwards, the city did something amazing: they raised the whole city up by several metres and built a giant sea wall to protect it.

🌀 Hurricane Katrina, USA (2005)

Katrina grew into a huge Category 5 storm over the Gulf of Mexico before hitting New Orleans as a Category 3. When the city's flood walls broke, water filled 80% of the city. Thousands of people were rescued from rooftops by helicopter and boat. The disaster changed how America prepares for storms.

🌏 Typhoon Haiyan, Philippines (2013)

One of the most powerful storms ever to hit land! Haiyan had winds of 315 kilometres per hour when it smashed into the Philippines. It pushed a storm surge — a huge dome of sea water — over coastal towns. Ships the size of buildings were left sitting in the middle of streets.

⚡ Amazing fact! Hurricanes spin in opposite directions depending on where they form! In the Northern Hemisphere they spin anticlockwise, and in the Southern Hemisphere they spin clockwise — all because Earth is turning underneath them.
