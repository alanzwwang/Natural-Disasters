🌋 Natural Disasters 🌪️

From fiery volcanic eruptions to deep-sea tsunamis and mighty mudslides — explore 10 of Earth's most powerful natural events!

👋 Welcome, brave explorer!

Our planet is amazing — but sometimes it can be wild! Earthquakes shake the ground, volcanoes blast melted rock into the sky, tsunamis race across the ocean faster than jet planes, and mudslides turn whole hillsides into rivers of mud.

This website teaches you about 10 different types of natural disasters. Every disaster has its own page with three slides: first Facts & History, then Pictures, and finally Famous Disasters from the past. At the bottom of every slide is a Picture Sources section showing exactly where each photo came from. Pick a disaster below and start your adventure!
