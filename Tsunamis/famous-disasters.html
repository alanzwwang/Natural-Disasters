🌊 Famous Tsunamis

Tsunamis — Slide 3 of 3: Famous Disasters

These are some of the most famous — and most powerful — tsunamis in history. Each one taught people something new about how to stay safe!

🌏 Indian Ocean Tsunami (2004)

On December 26, 2004, a gigantic magnitude 9.1 earthquake under the sea near Indonesia started a tsunami that raced across the whole Indian Ocean. Waves hit 14 countries, from Indonesia all the way to Africa. It was one of the worst natural disasters in modern history, and afterwards many countries joined together to build a warning system for the Indian Ocean too.

🇯🇵 Japan Tsunami (2011)

After the huge Tōhoku earthquake, a tsunami with waves up to 40 metres tall — as high as a 13-storey building — smashed into the coast of Japan. Sea walls that were built to protect towns were overtopped. The disaster taught engineers to build even higher walls and better escape routes.

🌋 Krakatoa Tsunami (1883)

When the volcano Krakatoa exploded in 1883, it pushed up tsunamis as tall as 40 metres that swept around the islands of Indonesia. The waves were so powerful they carried a whole ship almost 3 kilometres inland and left it in the jungle!

⚡ Amazing fact! In 1958, a landslide crashed into Lituya Bay in Alaska and created the tallest wave ever recorded — a "mega-tsunami" 524 metres tall, taller than the Empire State Building! Amazingly, a fisherman in a boat rode the wave and survived.
